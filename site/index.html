GOOD QUESTIONS

A little
more clarity.

What is SpicyCalc?

SpicyCalc is an iPhone app with educational calculation tools, personal records, and wellness tracking. It brings number-based tools and daily logs into one place.

Does SpicyCalc tell me what to take?

No. It performs arithmetic from values you enter. It does not select substances, recommend amounts, or create treatment plans. Medical decisions belong with a licensed healthcare professional.

Do I need an account?

No SpicyCalc account is required. Your personal records are stored locally on your device. See the app privacy policy for details.

How much does it cost?

Check the App Store for the current price and purchase details in your region. SpicyCalc is designed around access without a recurring subscription.

Where can I get support?

Visit the SpicyCalc support page or email support@ocoeestudios.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.
